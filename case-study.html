Smart dairy

Mimba Smart Dairy

Improved operational efficiency by 30% through clearer dairy farm management workflows.

Role Product design, UI design, prototyping
Timeline Project work
Impact +30% operational efficiency

Overview Problem Process Solution Results Takeaways

Overview

What changed

Mimba needed a clearer way to manage dairy farm operations across web and mobile. Omar designed high-fidelity interfaces for inventory tracking, milk production monitoring, herd health dashboards, and farm management.

Problem

The product needed operational clarity across daily farm work.

Dairy operations depend on fast access to inventory, production, and herd health information. The challenge was to turn those details into an intuitive product experience for farmers and operations teams.

Process

From workflow needs to interface structure.

01

Mapped the main dairy farm workflows and information needs.

02

Designed web and mobile screens for monitoring and daily management.

03

Collaborated with product and development partners to refine the solution.

Solution

Make operational data easier to understand and act on.

The final design emphasized clear dashboards, real-time inventory visibility, milk production monitoring, and herd health views, giving users a more intuitive product for day-to-day farm management.

Results

Measured impact after launch.

+30% operational efficiency
Web farm management app
Mobile farm management app

Key takeaways

What this project proved.

  • Operational products need dashboards that support real daily decisions.
  • High-fidelity prototypes help teams align before build work gets expensive.
  • Good product design turns complex workflows into clear, repeatable actions.
