Process

A practical process for making products easier to use.

01

Research the context

Use surveys, interviews, personas, and usability testing to understand users, constraints, and business goals.

02

Map the structure

Build user flows, journey maps, and information architecture so the experience has a clear product logic.

03

Prototype the solution

Create wireframes, high-fidelity interfaces, interaction details, and design system components for testing and handoff.

04

Improve with the team

Collaborate with developers, QA, product managers, and stakeholders to refine, ship, and improve the product after feedback.
